# Scene Rules

Every scene entry in `project/scene-plan.md` must use this template:

```text
Scene NN — short title
Time: HH:MM:SS.mmm–HH:MM:SS.mmm (timeline start/end)
Narration: exact phrase, paragraph, or beat covered (do not rewrite voiceover)
Characters: approved names or None
Props: approved assets
Pose: starting pose and any required later poses
Movement: exact action and time/cue; state what remains still
Camera: locked, or exact approved change
Text: exact on-screen copy, entry time, exit time, and placement
Colors: specify white/red/limited yellow use
SFX: cue and time, or None
Transition: type and duration
End frame: exact composition at scene end
QC notes: spelling, continuity, sync, texture, and readability risks
```

Rules:
- Scene times must be contiguous unless the approved plan explicitly preserves a gap; together they must cover the intended timeline without accidental overlap.
- Tie actions to audio timecodes or a clearly identified narration beat. The narration remains unchanged.
- Group dialogue by visual action or horror beat rather than making one scene per sentence.
- Avoid vague instructions. State what changes, when it changes, and what stays still.
- The first pilot is only the first approved 15–20 seconds. Do not invent later scenes or render the complete video during planning.
