# QC Checklist

Before approving any preview:
- [ ] Render dimensions, frame rate, duration, and codec match the requested preview/export.
- [ ] Narration is the original recording; duration, pitch, speed, words, and pauses are unchanged.
- [ ] Visual action follows the approved narration beat and does not invent unapproved story content.
- [ ] All on-screen text is exact, correctly spelled, readable, and held long enough.
- [ ] Characters and props match approved designs and remain consistent.
- [ ] Background is genuinely black; white/red/limited yellow palette is respected; no green or gradients.
- [ ] Camera is static unless the plan says otherwise; movement is restrained and stiff, not polished cartoon motion.
- [ ] VHS/CRT treatment affects text and figures, remains restrained, and does not harm readability.
- [ ] No unnecessary objects, flashes, or unplanned transitions appear.
- [ ] Inspect representative frames at scene entrance, main action, text hold, transition, and ending.
- [ ] Review the preview with audio, not only the contact sheet.
- [ ] Record known issues and fix blocking issues before approving the next batch.
- [ ] Confirm private WAV/script files and rendered video are not staged for Git.
